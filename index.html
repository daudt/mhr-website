Who We Are

Run With Us

We are an Erie, Colorado based running group built on training and friendship. Whether you're a front-runner or a back of the packer, we provide a group training environment that will enable you to improve.

We hope you will join us in 2026 and allow us to help you reach your fitness goals! Group training can be a powerful catalyst for positive change. Come check us out!
